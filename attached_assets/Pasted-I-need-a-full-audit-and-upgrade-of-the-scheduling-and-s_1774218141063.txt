I need a full audit and upgrade of the scheduling and shift marketplace system so it functions more like Schedulefly / HotSchedules style workforce scheduling, while still being scalable from small business to enterprise.

Primary goal:
Build a rules-based shift marketplace where workers only see and can request shifts they are actually eligible to pick up. The marketplace must support schedule publishing, shift exchange, approvals, alerts, permissions, and auditability.

Core business rules:
1. A marketplace shift should only be visible to an employee or contractor if they match the required eligibility rules.
2. Minimum default eligibility rules:
   - same company
   - same department
   - same branch
   - same employee group
3. The system should be extensible to also support:
   - same position/role
   - same pay class
   - certifications/licenses
   - no schedule conflict
   - no leave conflict
   - no overtime/max hours violation
   - minimum rest-period rules
4. A scheduled shift remains the responsibility of the originally assigned employee/contractor until:
   - another eligible worker has requested or accepted it,
   - all required approvals are completed,
   - the schedule is officially updated in the system.
5. The UI must clearly display that responsibility warning wherever a shift is listed or offered.
