1. Enterprise HR Hierarchy Model
The modern enterprise HR hierarchy has three layers of structure.
Enterprise
   ↓
Company
   ↓
Division / Business Unit
   ↓
Branch / Location
   ↓
Department
   ↓
Position
   ↓
Employee
Separate from this is the reporting hierarchy:
Employee
   ↓
Supervisor
   ↓
Manager
   ↓
Executive
So the system has two parallel structures:
Organizational structure (business structure)
Reporting structure (authority structure)
