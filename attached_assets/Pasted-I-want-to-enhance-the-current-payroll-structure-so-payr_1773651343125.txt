I want to enhance the current payroll structure so payroll disbursements can be tracked by both payment method and source/funding account, with year-to-date totals and reporting.

Goal:
Build a payroll payment tracking layer that separates payroll calculation from payroll disbursement. I need to track how net payroll was paid and which account it came from, including YTD totals by payment method and funding account.

- PayPal should use PayPal or linked funding sources
- Venmo should use Venmo or linked funding sources
- Cash App should use Cash App or linked funding sources
