Payroll System Components (Implementation Summary)
A payroll system needs several structured components that work together to calculate employee pay.
1. Pay Codes
Pay Codes define the type of time being recorded.
They categorize hours entered by employees.
Examples
REGULAR
OVERTIME
DOUBLE_TIME
VACATION
SICK
HOLIDAY
UNPAID_LEAVE
Purpose
classify time entries
determine which pay formula to apply
connect time to payroll calculations
Example:
Employee Timesheet
8 hours → REGULAR
2 hours → OVERTIME
2. Pay Formulas
Pay Formulas define how pay is calculated.
Core formulas
REGULAR_PAY = hours * hourly_rate

OVERTIME_PAY = hours * hourly_rate * 1.5

DOUBLE_TIME_PAY = hours * hourly_rate * 2

VACATION_PAY = hours * hourly_rate

SALARY_PAY = annual_salary / pay_periods
Example calculation:
Regular hours: 8
Overtime hours: 2
Rate: $20

Regular pay = 8 × 20 = $160
Overtime pay = 2 × 20 × 1.5 = $60
Total = $220
3. Pay Stub Accounts
Pay Stub Accounts represent line items on the paycheck.
Categories include:

Overtime Wages
Vacation Pay
Bonus
Employee Taxes
Federal Income Tax
Social Security
Medicare
State Income Tax
SDI
Employer Taxes
Employer Social Security
Employer Medicare
FUTA
State Unemployment
Deductions
Health Insurance
401k
Garnishments
4. Policy Groups
Policy Groups bundle labor rules applied to employees.
Example:
Full-Time Policy Group
• overtime after 8 hours/day
• double time after 12 hours/day
• meal break rules
• vacation accrual
Policy groups allow rules to apply to many employees at once.
5. Payroll Calculation Flow
Typical payroll workflow:
Employee time entry
      ↓
Pay Code
      ↓
Pay Formula
      ↓
Pay Stub Account
      ↓
Payroll totals
      ↓
Tax deductions
      ↓
Net pay
6. Example Data Structure (Simplified)
class PayCode:
    name
    formula

class PayFormula:
    name
    calculation

class PayStubAccount:
    name
    category  # earnings, tax, deduction

class EmployeeTimeEntry:
    employee_id
    hours
    pay_code
7. Example Payroll Calculation (Pseudo Code)
for entry in timesheet:
    pay_formula = get_formula(entry.pay_code)
    pay_amount = pay_formula(entry.hours, employee.rate)
    add_to_pay_stub(employee, pay_amount)
