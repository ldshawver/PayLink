Policies control how employee time is interpreted, adjusted, and converted into payroll and leave balances.
Each employee should be assigned a Policy Group, which links to all relevant policies.
Core Architecture
Recommended calculation flow:
Employee Time Entry
      ↓
Schedule Policy
      ↓
Rounding Policy
      ↓
Meal & Break Policies
      ↓
Regular Time Policy
      ↓
Overtime Policy
      ↓
Premium Policy
      ↓
Exception Policy
      ↓
Holiday Policy
      ↓
Accrual Policy
      ↓
Payroll Calculation
Policy Categories
1. Recurring Holidays
Defines company holidays that repeat each year.
Examples:
New Year's Day
Independence Day
Thanksgiving
Christmas
System requirements:
store holiday date
support recurring yearly holidays
apply holiday pay rules
integrate with Holiday Policies
Example logic:
if date == holiday:
   apply holiday policy
2. Schedule Policies
Defines expected work schedules.
Purpose:
determine expected work hours
detect lateness
detect missed shifts
Example configuration:
Shift Start: 9:00 AM
Shift End: 5:00 PM
Expected Hours: 8
Grace Period: 5 minutes
System functions:
compare clock-in times vs scheduled shift
generate attendance exceptions
3. Rounding Policies
Controls how punch times are rounded.
Example rule:
round to nearest 15 minutes
Example behavior:
8:03 → 8:00
8:08 → 8:15
System requirements:
configurable rounding increments
apply during time processing
4. Meal Policies
Defines required unpaid meal breaks.
Example (California compliant):
Meal break after 5 hours
Duration: 30 minutes
System functions:
enforce meal break requirements
automatically deduct meals if configured
generate violations if missed
5. Break Policies
Defines paid rest breaks.
Example:
10-minute break every 4 hours worked
System requirements:
track break punches
enforce minimum break rules
6. Regular Time Policies
Defines what counts as regular work hours.
Example:
Regular hours = first 8 hours per day
System functions:
classify worked hours as regular time
feed into overtime calculations
7. Overtime Policies
Defines overtime rules.
Example (California):
Overtime after 8 hours/day
Double time after 12 hours/day
Overtime after 40 hours/week
System functions:
calculate overtime thresholds
convert regular hours to overtime hours
8. Premium Policies
Defines additional pay conditions.
Examples:
night shift premium
weekend premium
holiday premium
Example rule:
Night shift premium = +10% pay
System requirements:
detect premium conditions
apply additional pay multipliers
9. Exception Policies
Handles rule violations.
Examples:
late clock-in
early clock-out
missed break
missed meal
System functions:
flag exceptions
notify supervisors
generate reports
10. Accrual Policies
Defines how leave is earned.
Examples:
Vacation accrual:
1 hour per 30 hours worked
Alternative:
4 hours PTO per pay period
System requirements:
calculate accrual balances
enforce accrual caps
apply policy based on employee type
11. Absence Policies
Defines allowed absence rules.
Examples:
unpaid leave
sick leave
personal leave
System functions:
deduct hours from accrual accounts
track absence reason codes
12. Holiday Policies
Defines how holidays affect pay.
Possible rules:
Paid holiday
Holiday worked = overtime rate
Holiday worked = double time
System functions:
detect holiday dates
apply premium or leave rules
13. Accrual Accounts
Tracks balances of earned time off.
Typical accounts:
vacation
sick leave
PTO
comp time
Example account structure:
employee_id
accrual_type
balance_hours
accrual_rate
max_balance
System functions:
update balances automatically
deduct when leave is used
Policy Group System
Employees should be assigned to Policy Groups.
Example groups:
Full Time
Part Time
Managers
Contractors
Each group links to:
schedule policy
overtime policy
accrual policy
break policy
meal policy
This allows consistent rule application.
Core Payroll Engine Requirements
PayLink must support:
• rule-based time classification
• automated overtime calculations
• accrual balance tracking
• holiday detection
• attendance exception tracking
• policy inheritance via policy groups
Simplified Policy Engine Model
Example object structure:
PolicyGroup
  ├ SchedulePolicy
  ├ RoundingPolicy
  ├ MealPolicy
  ├ BreakPolicy
  ├ RegularTimePolicy
  ├ OvertimePolicy
  ├ PremiumPolicy
  ├ ExceptionPolicy
  ├ HolidayPolicy
  ├ AccrualPolicy
Key Goal for Replit Implementation
Ensure the system:
Applies policies in the correct order
Allows policies to be assigned per employee group
Keeps policy logic separate from payroll calculations
Supports future rule expansion
