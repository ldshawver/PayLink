Prompt for Replit — Implement Secondary Wage Groups in PayLink
You are implementing a payroll and workforce management system for PayLink. The system must support Secondary Wage Groups, a feature used in advanced payroll platforms such as TimeTrex.
Your task is to implement this feature correctly so employees can be paid different wage rates depending on the job, task, or role they perform during a shift.
Concept Explanation
A Secondary Wage Group allows an employee to earn a different hourly rate than their primary wage when performing specific tasks or roles.
Employees normally have a Primary Wage Rate, but sometimes they perform work that should be paid at a higher or different rate.
The system must support multiple wage rates for the same employee.
Example
Employee:
Employee: John
Primary Job: Warehouse Worker
Primary Wage: $20/hour
Secondary wage roles:
Supervisor Duties → $25/hour
Forklift Operator → $23/hour
Training Instructor → $28/hour
If John clocks into a shift using the Supervisor wage group, payroll should calculate:
Hours worked: 4
Rate: $25/hour
Pay: $100
Even though his normal wage is $20/hour.
How Secondary Wage Groups Are Used
Secondary wage groups can be triggered by:
• job assignment
• pay code
• shift type
• station
• manual override
Example workflow:
Employee clocks into job
System checks assigned wage group
Payroll uses that wage rate
Example Time Entry
Employee: John

8:00 - 12:00 → Warehouse Worker ($20)
12:00 - 16:00 → Supervisor ($25)
Payroll calculation:
Warehouse: 4 × $20 = $80
Supervisor: 4 × $25 = $100

Total Pay = $180

else:

pay = hours_worked * pay_rate
