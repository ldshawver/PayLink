Core Modules

Built for Real Workforce Operations

Every module works together seamlessly — from the time clock to the paycheck.

💰

Payroll Processing

Multi-step tax wizard, NACHA ACH direct deposit, contractor invoicing, pay stubs, and full audit trail. Handles hourly, salaried, and owner distributions.

⏱

Time & Attendance

Kiosk time clock, GPS-optional punches, daily and double overtime, station enforcement, and automatic timesheet generation with approval workflows.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Drag-and-drop schedule builder, shift marketplace for swaps, recurring templates, time-off integration, and publish-with-notify for employees.

👥

Employee Management

Complete HR profiles with contacts, wages, documents, skills, certifications, and self-service portal. Supports 7 worker group types.

🏢

Multi-Company

Manage multiple companies, divisions, cost centers, and departments under one enterprise. Universal entities shared across companies.

📊

Reports & Tax Forms

W-2, 1099-NEC, 941, 940, DE 9, DE 9C, 1096 generation. Employee, timesheet, payroll, expense, and job cost reports with CSV export.
