Enterprise Security

Security That Belongs in the Enterprise

Payroll data, SSNs, bank account numbers, and wage history are among the most sensitive data your business holds. PayLink is built with enterprise-grade security at every layer — not added as an afterthought.

PROTOCOL ACTIVE

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Three enforced roles — Admin, Manager, and Employee — with granular permissions at every API endpoint. Managers cannot see data outside their company. Employees cannot access pay rates or HR records for others. Every route is independently protected.

0x52424143 · 3-LAYER
ISOLATION VERIFIED

Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Every record in the database is scoped to a company. No query returns data from another tenant. Company ID is enforced server-side on every read and write — frontend filters alone are never trusted.

0x49534F4C · TENANT-SCOPE
SESSION HARDENED

Encrypted Sessions & Secure Cookies

Sessions are stored server-side in PostgreSQL via a hardened session store. Cookies are httpOnly, Secure, and SameSite-protected. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 10). No plain-text credentials ever stored.

0x53455353 · BCRYPT-10
AUDIT LOGGING

Complete Authorization Audit Log

Every sensitive action — payroll runs, schedule changes, document access, approvals, configuration changes — is written to an immutable audit log. Who did what, when, and from where. Required for enterprise compliance audits.

0x41554449 · IMMUTABLE
LEGAL HOLD ACTIVE

Document Security & Legal Hold

HR documents support access control lists (ACLs), retention policies, and legal hold. Documents under hold cannot be deleted regardless of user role. A separate document audit log tracks every view, download, and modification.

0x444F4353 · ACL-GUARD
BANKING SHIELD

Banking Data Protection

Bank account routing numbers and account numbers for direct deposit are protected by authenticated endpoints with strict employee-scoping. No unauthenticated endpoint can access financial data. ACH files are generated server-side and never exposed to frontend storage.

0x42414E4B · ACH-SECURE
