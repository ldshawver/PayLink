Features

Everything Your Workforce Needs

From the time clock to the paycheck — every module works together in one integrated platform.

💰

Payroll Processing

Multi-step tax wizard with federal and state calculations. Generate pay stubs, process direct deposit via NACHA ACH files, and handle complex tax scenarios including California-specific requirements.

⏱

Time & Attendance

Kiosk-mode time clock with PIN login, station enforcement, GPS tracking, and automatic overtime calculations. Supports daily, weekly, and double overtime with configurable policies.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Visual schedule builder with drag-and-drop, recurring templates, shift publishing with email/SMS notifications, and automatic conflict detection with time-off requests.

🔄

Shift Marketplace

HotSchedules-style shift swapping. Employees post shifts for pickup, others request them, and managers approve — with automatic eligibility checks for conflicts, hours, and rest periods.

👥

Employee Management

Complete HR profiles: contacts, wages, pay methods, documents, skills, certifications, education, languages, and memberships. Self-service portal for employees to manage their own data.

🏢

Multi-Company

True enterprise architecture. Manage unlimited companies, divisions, cost centers, departments, and positions under one login. Share universal entities or keep them company-specific.

📊

Tax Form Generation

Generate W-2, 1099-NEC, 941, 940, DE 9, DE 9C, and 1096 forms. Comprehensive reporting with employee, timesheet, payroll, expense, and job cost reports — all exportable to CSV.

🧾

Expense Management

Receipt tracking with AI-powered scanning (OCR extracts vendor, items, costs, tax, and payment method). Approval workflows, job cost allocation, and check printing.

📄

Contractor Invoicing

Contractors submit invoices through their portal. Track by job, approve, and process payments alongside regular payroll. 1099-NEC generation at year end.

🏦

ACH Direct Deposit

Generate NACHA-formatted ACH files for each payroll run. Upload to your bank to initiate all direct deposits. Workers without bank info automatically default to check.

📜

Policy Engine

Comprehensive policy management covering overtime, accruals, breaks, time-off, and more. Quick setup for California-compliant defaults with full customization.

🔍

Payroll Audit

Automated audit engine scans for missing EINs, SSNs, classification mismatches, contractor deduction errors, missing tax setup, and volunteer time entries before you process.
